# Wild Soft
_Wild Soft I_ emerges from a sustained, personal relationship with the natural world. These images trace a cyclical passage through the seasons, shaped largely by the landscape of West Yorkshire. The work reflects time spent observing growth, decay, and renewal, allowing nature to be encountered as both environment and presence. A smaller section, made during time in the lush greens of Thailand, offers a contrasting rhythm and beauty.
